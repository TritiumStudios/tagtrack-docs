## Product Specification Sheet

The **Ewing Aerospace BlueLRS 2.4 GHz TX Module** is a high-power radio transmitter designed for professional UAS and long-range FPV systems. Utilizing blueLRS, our own privately maintained ELRS firmware fork, we can ensure optimum performance and maintain compatibility with NDAA compliant hardware. It offers wide voltage support, selectable packet rate and power and a companion lua script for easy runtime access to settings.
